snakeitbaby

Meduza's built-in video editor, hosted on GitHub Pages.

Import files from your device into a project. Projects and imported media stay in this browser profile. Export downloads the finished video.

Open-source credits

The editing engine is adapted from OpenCut Classic, copyright 2025–2026 OpenCut, under the MIT license. Meduza adds the media library, Telegram intake, local export saving, and workspace integration.
